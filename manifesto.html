The Cannabis Manifesto

Doc Strange · Christian P Taylor · 2026

"Now at last I am free to be me.
I want to be free, and to free others."

I Am Not Being Pushed Around Anymore

For 25 years I self-medicated. I didn't know why. I knew I needed it. I knew that without it I couldn't string a sentence together, couldn't sit still, couldn't focus, couldn't perform, couldn't be the person I knew I was. I just didn't have the language for it.

Then I got a diagnosis. AuDHD. Then I got a prescription. Twenty minutes. About fifty pounds. Legal. Done.

And everything I had always known about myself was confirmed in a doctor's office in less time than it takes to watch a TV show.

I am not being pushed around anymore. Not by an industry that kept this medicine underground. Not by a culture that looked at people like me and saw a problem instead of a person. Not by anyone who benefits from keeping the people who need this medicine confused, ashamed, or uninformed.

What I Know

I know that there are people right now — thousands of them, probably millions — who are where I was. Self-medicating without knowing why. Agitated, dismissed, told they're mental. Dragging themselves through a life that doesn't fit their brain, without the one thing that would let them function.

I know that the prescription exists. That it is accessible. That it works.

I know because I am living proof.

My skin was like scales. Now it isn't. I couldn't sit at a desk for more than a few minutes without forcing myself. Now I can build entire ecosystems. I couldn't articulate what was in my head. Now — medicated, with the right tools beside me — I can build the things that need to be built.

Cannabis did that. Not a lifestyle choice. Medicine. My medicine. Prescribed, documented, legal.

"I've built all this so people don't keep looking at me like I'm fucking mental."

— Doc Strange

What The Industry Is Missing

Some of the loudest voices in the cannabis industry are mumbling. They know the plant. They don't know how to talk to the people who need it most. They speak to the converted. They shout at each other. They brand and market and campaign — and the person sitting at home, skin on fire, brain on fire, who has been self-medicating for twenty years and doesn't know why it helps, never hears them.

I can speak to that person. Because I was that person.

I am a performer. I have held rooms since I was five years old. I am not an influencer. I am not a content creator. I am a bridge — between the plant and the patient, between the underground that kept this medicine alive and the mainstream audience that is only now being allowed to talk about it.

The Mission

Travel the world. Meet the growers. Feel the earth. Sit in the places where this plant has been grown, used, revered, hidden, protected. Tell those stories honestly, without a brand filter, without a corporate agenda.

Germany first. Then Spain. Then wherever the earth is doing the work — California, Morocco, wherever the lineage lives.

Write the book. Cannabis Confidential. In the spirit of Anthony Bourdain — who went to the source, told the truth, and trusted the reader to handle it.

Build the ecosystem that helps every patient who comes after me find what I found. The base level. The foundation. The place from which it becomes possible to contribute, to connect, to get through this game of life.

Because everybody else treats it like a game. Everybody except the people being hurt by it the most.
