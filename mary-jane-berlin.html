Why I'm Going

I spent 42 years self-medicating. Nobody told me there was a legal route. When I finally got prescribed, nobody told me what the strains did, what the terpenes meant, or how to find the right one. So I built Cannabin-Oid — the thing that should have existed.


Mary Jane Berlin is Europe's biggest cannabis expo. I'm going to meet the people who grow it, who sell it, who run clubs for it, who build the technology around it — and I'm going to tell them that there's a platform that connects them all. Built by a patient. Free to join. No corporate agenda.


If you've got a problem with cannabis being discussed openly — with the science of terpenes and the politics of prescription — move along. This is medicine. My medicine. And I'm proud of it.
