Who they are

"I was kept in the dark about my own medicine. Nobody told me anything. So I built Cannabin-Oid, because that's what I do — I make the invisible visible."

Chris P Taylor — known as Chris P Tee, or just Doc — is a variety entertainer and community builder from Bristol. They've been performing since they were seventeen. Magician. Hypnotist. Punch & Judy. Puppets. Characters that emerge when the moment calls for them.

They're also a legal medical cannabis patient, prescribed via Alternaleaf UK. AuDHD. They/them. Running on their autistic side these days — thoughtful, chill, but with a few things to say about being kept in the dark and fed with bullshit. Like fungus.

They built Cannabin-Oid because when they were first prescribed, nobody told them anything useful. No guidance. No community. No tool that should have existed. So they built one.
