★ Founding Hamlet Motor-Oid Village GlowGadgets PLUR

GlowGadgets started at the tail end of Bristol's rave scene — glow sticks and gear sold to ravers who wanted something to hold onto through a set. Doc's still around: DnB Santa now, same crowd, same ethos.

No dealer step, no portal fees — buyers and sellers meet direct, same as the scene always ran on word of mouth. Motor-Oid is that same idea applied to cars: sell direct, no middleman, no performance required.
