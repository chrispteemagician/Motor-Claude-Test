How we found him

Mary Jane Berlin, 2026. Thousands of people. Hundreds of stands. Cannabis genetics, vaporisers, compliance tech, lawyers, grow equipment. The usual.

And then: a beekeeper. With a stand. Selling honey named after cannabis strains.

Not because he was trying to be clever. Because Tamás Megyes makes gourmet honey from freeze-dried fruits, tonka beans, vanilla and gourmet spices — and he named each variety after the flavour profile it reminded him of. Strawberry Cough. Blue Dream.

The man knows what he's doing.

We brought the Blue Dream home. Someone tasted it and said: "It tastes just like my dad's honey." She was tasting the character — Frühtrachthonig, the first honey of the season. Early blossom. Naturally mild. That's what her dad tasted of too.

Give me a clue 🎈
You know how some people just belong in a room without needing to explain why? That. Tamás didn't show up at Mary Jane Berlin to be alternative or edgy. He showed up because his honey genuinely belongs in the same world as terpenes and flavour profiles and people who pay attention to what goes into their bodies. The industry recognised its own.

Regional. Honest. Exquisite.

Tamás runs Imkerei Megyes from Wendeburg, near Braunschweig in Lower Saxony. Small operation. High standards. The honey is produced where the bees are, from what the land actually grows.

No shortcuts. No bulk blending. No mystery ingredients. Regional means it comes from somewhere specific. Honest means you know exactly what's in it. Exquisite means he wasn't willing to settle for anything less.

That's the whole philosophy. It fits on three words because it doesn't need more than three words.
